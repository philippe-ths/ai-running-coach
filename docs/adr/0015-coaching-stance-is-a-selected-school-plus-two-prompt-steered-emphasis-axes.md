# Coaching stance is a runner-selected school plus two pack-carried, prompt-steered emphasis axes, with no free-text hatch

P1.2 (ADR 0014) built the `Coaching corpus` substrate end-to-end but wired its school to one hardcoded `DEFAULT_SCHOOL_ID` (aerobic-base). P1.3 makes the corpus runner-controllable by shipping `Coaching stance`: the runner's two operable controls over how the coach reasons. Stance bundles two things the design (`docs/vision/p1-voice-and-stance.md` §6) already settled as resolved: a **selected school of thought** (which philosophy the coach grounds its judgment in) and **two emphasis axes** — Data ↔ Sentiment and Process ↔ Outcome — that are content-focus, orthogonal to `Voice`. This ADR decides how those two controls reach the model and where the milestone draws its boundary, mirroring how voice (ADR 0012/0013) and the corpus (ADR 0014) were handled.

**The decision.** Coaching stance is a runner-declared **selected school** plus **two operable 1-5 emphasis axes**, stored as nullable columns on `coaching_relationship` (the voice precedent), resolved at read time to a `StanceProfile` (the `resolve_voice` precedent), and reaching the model through **two different seams under one new prompt id**:

1. **The selected school rides the existing corpus seam — no new mechanism.** The runner's `stance_school` simply replaces the hardcoded `DEFAULT_SCHOOL_ID` threaded into `_build_corpus_context`, so the chosen school's principles and method-framing arrive in the existing `corpus` pack section that `coach_message_v4`'s rule 25 already consumes. School selection alone needs no new prompt rule; the P1.2 substrate already reasons over whatever school is in the section.

2. **The two emphasis axes ride the context pack as data, steered by a new explicit prompt rule.** They are net-new — they exist nowhere in the corpus, prompt, or schema today. They are surfaced as a small new `stance` pack section (the axis values with their pole labels), kept *separate* from the `corpus` section because the corpus section is coaching *knowledge* (house principles + school) while the emphasis axes are *runner preference*. A new two-stage prompt **`coach_message_v5` = `coach_message_v4` + a static emphasis addendum (prompt rule 26)** instructs the coach to honour the named axes; the axis values themselves stay in the pack (only the rule lives in the prompt, exactly as rule 25 references the `corpus` section). This follows the `Vn = V(n-1) + addendum` idiom; v5 inherits two-stage + voice + corpus from v4 and predecessors stay byte-stable.

3. **The emphasis axes get explicit, axis-named prompt steering rather than implicit reliance on the corpus rule.** Research Q3 (`p1-voice-and-stance.md`) found explicit, axis-named instruction steers far better than a generic "use the corpus" line, and the deterministic eval gate cannot measure emphasis quality (that is the deferred #164 judge), so explicit prompt steering is the only reliable lever. A new prompt id is the cost; it is the same cost school selection already carries (the corpus is inert until v4+ is active) and rides the battle-tested versioned-cache / async-regen / display-safe machinery (#260/#261/#264).

4. **The authority tier is unchanged and re-enforced for the new axis.** Stance reweights emphasis and method-framing only; it never licenses unsupported advice, grounds a fact, or overrides this run's re-derived `DerivedMetric` or the safety floor (`Authority tiering`). This holds in three layers exactly as the corpus does: prompt rule 26, the existing narrow validator rules (which fire regardless of what steered the output — stance introduces no new evidence-laundering path because the emphasis axes are runner dials, not citable text), and the cross-school invariance eval extended to vary by the runner's stored stance.

5. **No free-text stance hatch.** Voice ships a free-text hatch because tone can never move facts or safety. A stance free-text hatch would be runner-supplied coaching *content* that steers advice — which is exactly `User materials` (P4), a deferred concept with its own untrusted-input store and the hardest authority tier (user materials beat house philosophy). P1.3 stays selection + dials only, so it carries no untrusted input and `aiw-security-testing` is out of scope. A runner who wants a philosophy outside the house schools is the motivating case for P4, not a reason to improvise a mini-User-materials in P1.3.

## Considered options

- **Emphasis axes as pure pack data honoured by the existing corpus rule (no new prompt id).** Rejected: the cheapest path, but the steering would be implicit and weak — research Q3 shows a generic "use the corpus" line with two numeric dials buried in a section steers unreliably, and the deterministic eval cannot catch weak emphasis steering. The whole point of stance is that emphasis *visibly* shifts; implicit steering undercuts the feature's reason to exist.
- **Emphasis axes as a system-prompt persona block, mirroring voice's `render_voice_block`.** Rejected: it would place stance *content* in the system prompt rather than the pack, breaking the "stance content rides the pack" symmetry ADR 0014 set deliberately to keep retrieved knowledge on the data-the-coach-reasons-over side. The emphasis axes belong in the pack as data; only the rule that references them belongs in the prompt.
- **Fold the emphasis axes into the existing `corpus` pack section.** Rejected: it conflates two different kinds of thing — the corpus section is coaching knowledge (house principles + the keyed school), the emphasis axes are runner preference. A separate `stance` section keeps the concepts clean and keeps the `corpus` section's P1.2 shape (now varying only by selected school).
- **Add a `stance_freetext` escape-hatch for voice-parity.** Rejected: it is the `User materials` (P4) surface by definition — runner-supplied content that steers advice — and would prematurely open the untrusted-input / prompt-injection surface and the hardest authority tier that ADR 0014 explicitly deferred to a store designed against P4's constraints.
- **Activate v5 in prod via the repo config default.** Rejected: the repo default stays `coach_report_v10`; activation is a deliberate Railway env flip (`COACH_PROMPT_ID=coach_message_v5`) so rollback stays a zero-code config change, consistent with every Phase-1 milestone. (The owner ratified that P1.3 includes performing the prod flip as part of the milestone; the flip itself is an owner-authorized prod action.)

## Consequences

- P1.3 ships stance columns on `coaching_relationship` (+ a zero-backfill migration off `e1f2a3b4c5d6`), a `resolve_stance` in a stance domain module, a `_resolve_stance_for_activity` in `service.py` threaded into the three `build_context_pack` call sites, a `school_id` thread into `_build_corpus_context`, a new `stance` pack section + `_build_stance_context`, `coach_message_v5` (= v4 + rule 26) with a `STANCE_PROMPT_IDS` gate, `GET`/`PUT /api/coach/stance` + `schemas/stance.py`, a frontend stance panel, and two additional house schools (strength-led, periodization) so the selector has a meaningful range.
- Because v5 carries a new `stance` pack section and a selected (rather than hardcoded) school, v5 reports legitimately regenerate (the M0 versioned-cache idiom); pre-stance history is retained.
- Activating v5 in prod jumps `coach_message_v3 → v5`, turning on corpus *and* emphasis together — a larger behavioural step than one version. The prod flip is owner-authorized and owner-executed; the slow/worst-case generation path is verified before the flip (the P1.1 activation lesson) and confirmed live after.
- If a later feature lets stance widen what philosophy can touch, or `User materials` (P4) brings runner-supplied coaching content and the hardest authority tier, this ADR and ADR 0014 must be reopened. `User materials` introduces an untrusted-input surface P1.3 does not, so `aiw-security-testing` applies to P4, not here.
